# Camera attachments implementation

The approved attachment sheet replaces the Photos/Documents dropdown with Camera, Photos and Documents tiles, warm Atelier surfaces and the olive camera action. Camera opens an external Android camera Activity and shows an oriented, bounded preview with Retake and Use photo. Photos/Documents retain their existing system pickers. Use photo enters the existing repository attachment path; Send remains explicit.

A ViewModel-owned controller binds each memory-only attempt to the displayed durable/runtime pair and current attachment readiness. Launch admission is consumed before starting the external Activity; rotation retains the attempt without relaunching. An opaque launch token is the only camera value saved through Activity recreation. Camera backgrounding is expected; backgrounding an unconfirmed review cancels it. Route exit, ViewModel clear, reload, disconnect and ownership loss discard the attempt. Late creation, preview and Activity results cannot revive a cancelled attempt or attach to another runtime.

The camera receives a temporary read/write FileProvider grant to one random JPEG under a dedicated app-private cache directory. No CAMERA or shared-storage permission, gallery write, backup path, host/protocol change, migration, automatic resend, telemetry or diagnostic payload is added. The provider is non-exported and exposes only that directory. Originals remain until the existing reader/upload job finishes, then are deleted and grants revoked on success, rejection, failure or cancellation. Startup removes abandoned captures from process death without deleting active attempts. Preview decoding is sampled to at most 1280 pixels on the longest edge and honors EXIF orientation; the existing 25 MB image bound still applies. No capture URI, original or preview is written into the vault, draft, transcript or snapshot. Filesystem deletion remains subject to Android cache semantics; an external camera app owns its own capture behavior.
